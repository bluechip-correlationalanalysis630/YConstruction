## 🏗️ About the project

YConstruction helps construction teams track and manage site defects. You record notes on your iPhone, and the software organizes these findings for your project team. It uses smart technology that runs right on your phone to identify issues instantly. You can then connect this data to building models for easy review.

## 📋 Requirements

Before you begin, ensure your computer meets these specifications:

*   Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   Processor: Intel Core i5 or equivalent processor.
*   Memory: 8 GB of RAM or more.
*   Storage: 500 MB of free disk space.
*   Network: An active internet connection for initial setup and synchronization.

Once you install the software, launch it from your desktop icon. You will need to create a profile to link your phone data to your computer.

3. Enter your email address and create a password.

## 🏢 How it works

The system uses three core parts to manage your reporting workflow.

### Voice-first reporting
You speak into your phone while walking the job site. Our software listens to your descriptions and converts them into organized text records. It identifies specific construction terms automatically.

### IFC-grounded structure
The data syncs directly with your project models. We use industry-standard Building Information Modeling files to map every report to a specific location or room in your project. This ensures your team knows exactly where the defect exists and what the issue involves.

### Review workflow
All reports appear within your workstation dashboard. You can group these items by floor, category, or status. Our Bonsai integration allows you to flag specific items for review in Blender. This creates a clear path for architects and engineers to verify repairs.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting common issues

If you encounter a problem, check the following solutions:

*   The sync process fails: Verify that both your phone and computer share the same internet network.
*   The application does not open: Ensure you installed the latest version from the releases page. Restart your computer if the installer hangs.
*   Voice recognition errors: Hold your phone close to your mouth when you record. Reduce background noise to improve the accuracy of the transcriptions.
*   Login issues: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to reset your credentials.

## 📊 Performance tips

To receive the best experience, keep these configurations in mind:

*   Clear your temporary cache folders once a month to keep the software running fast.
*   Enable push notifications so you get alerts when your team uploads new site reports.
*   Regularly update your building model files to ensure the software shows the latest design changes.
*   Keep your phone software updated to the latest version of iOS to ensure compatibility.

## 📁 Managing your data

All report data stays on your local machine for security. You choose when to sync this information to the cloud. You can export your reports into spreadsheets to share with project owners or subcontractors who do not use the application. Check the "Export" menu in the settings tab to see the available file formats, such as PDF and Excel.
